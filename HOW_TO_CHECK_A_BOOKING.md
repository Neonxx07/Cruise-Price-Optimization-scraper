# How to Check a Royal Caribbean / Celebrity Booking (ESPRESSO Portal)

A plain-English, step-by-step guide for a person doing this by hand — no cruise industry knowledge assumed. This is **not** about the automation tool; it's the manual process the automation is itself based on.

## A few words you'll see, explained

- **Category**: the type/location of the room (cabin) — e.g. Interior (no window), Ocean View, Balcony, Suite. Higher up this list = generally nicer and more expensive.
- **OBC (Onboard Credit)**: free money added to the guest's account to spend on the ship (drinks, shopping, excursions). Losing OBC is a real cost, even though no cash changes hands.
- **Package / Perk**: something bundled into the booking for free — a drink package, wifi, gratuities, etc.
- **WLT (Waitlisted)**: shown as available on paper, but not actually bookable yet. Treat it as unavailable.
- **Paid in Full**: the guest has already paid (or nearly paid) the whole balance. These bookings generally should not be repriced.

## Step-by-step

**1. Log in.**
Go to the ESPRESSO portal and log in with your username, password, and whatever second step (MFA code) it asks for.

**2. Search for the booking.**
Enter the reservation number in the search box and hit search. You'll land on the Reservation Summary page.

**3. Check the payment status first, before anything else.**
Look for "Total Price," "Payments Received," and "Final Payment Due." If the amount still due is very small compared to the total (or already $0 or negative), **stop here** — this booking is effectively already paid off and isn't worth repricing.

**4. Note the current category and current price.**
Write down (or keep visible) what category the guest is currently booked in and what the current total price is. You'll compare everything else against this.

**5. Click "Categories."**
This opens the table of every room category and whether it's available, and at what price.

**6. Look only at categories that are the SAME type or BETTER than the current one.**
Never suggest moving a guest to a "cheaper" category that's actually a worse room type — that's a downgrade, not a deal, even if it's less money. Only compare like-for-like or better.

**7. Ignore anything marked WLT (Waitlisted).**
It's not really available — don't try to move a guest there.

**8. Select a candidate category to preview it.**
Click the radio button next to the category you're considering. This just shows you a price comparison — **it does not change anything yet.**

**9. Read the comparison carefully before deciding anything:**
   - Is the new total price actually lower?
   - Did the OBC go down? If yes, the price drop needs to clearly outweigh the OBC lost — a small saving that costs a big chunk of OBC is not a real win.
   - Did the guest lose any package/perk (drink package, wifi, etc.)? If the perk they'd lose is worth more than what they'd save, it's not a real win either.
   - Did the guest lose a discount/promo code? Some of these can usually be re-added afterward by the agent (ones with names like "bonus," "promo," "loyalty," "email," or "sav" in them) — but check, don't assume.

**10. Only if it's a clear, genuine win — commit it.**
If you're confident the numbers work in the guest's favor, and only then, click **"Continue with New Rate."**

---

## ⚠️ THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MOST

**Never click "Continue with New Rate" (or "Continue") unless you are certain this is a real improvement for the guest and you mean to commit it right now.**

Everything up through selecting a category and viewing the comparison is just *looking* — nothing is saved or changed. The "Continue with New Rate" button is the only step that actually locks in a change to a real, live booking. If anything about the numbers looks unclear or you're not sure, stop and double-check (or ask someone) before clicking it — don't guess.

## Common things that LOOK like a good deal but usually aren't

- A lower price that came with a big OBC loss.
- A lower price that came with losing a perk (drink package, wifi, gratuities) worth more than the saving.
- A "cheaper" option that's actually a lower room category (a downgrade).
- A category showing a price but marked WLT (not really available).
- A booking that's already paid off, or close to it — repricing isn't worth pursuing.

## What a genuinely good deal looks like

- Same or better room category.
- Lower total price.
- OBC stayed the same or went up — or the price drop is clearly much bigger than any OBC lost.
- No lost perks, or only ones that can be re-added.
- The category is actually available (not WLT).

## Group bookings

Some bookings will show a page titled "Group Booking Summary" instead of "Reservation Summary" — this is normal, it works the same way, just don't be thrown off by the different title. You may also see two side-by-side price columns with two radio buttons in the category table (usually labeled things like "Best Rate" vs. "Best Value," or "Group Allocation" vs. "Group Prevailing") — check both sides, since the right-hand one occasionally shows a different price than the left.

## When in doubt

If the numbers don't clearly make sense, or something on the page looks unusual, don't act on it — stop and ask before touching "Continue with New Rate." A missed opportunity costs nothing; a wrong reprice affects a real customer's real booking.
